Appendix A — List of Important Formulas

Author

Longhai Li

Published

October 23, 2026

This appendix collects the boxed formulas of the chapters, together with the few companion formulas they rely on, sorted by topic. Each topic starts with its notation; the link after each formula leads to the section where it is derived. All designs draw units without replacement unless stated otherwise (UPSWR draws with replacement).

A.1 General Notation

Symbol Meaning
\(N\), \(n\) number of units in the population \(\mathcal U\) and in the sample \(\mathcal S\)
\(y_i\) value of the study variable for unit \(i\)
\(t = \sum_{i=1}^N y_i\), \(\ \bar y_U = t/N\) population total and population mean
\(S^2 = \frac{1}{N-1}\sum_{i=1}^N (y_i-\bar y_U)^2\) population variance
\(\bar y = \frac1n\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} y_i\), \(\ s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}(y_i-\bar y)^2\) sample mean and sample variance
\(1 - n/N\) finite population correction (fpc)
\(\hat V(\cdot)\), \(\ \mathrm{SE}(\cdot) = \sqrt{\hat V(\cdot)}\) estimated variance and standard error
\(z_{\alpha/2}\), \(\ t_{\alpha/2,\,df}\) upper \(\alpha/2\) quantiles of the standard normal and the \(t\) distribution

Confidence interval. For any estimator \(\hat\theta\) in this list, an approximate \(100(1-\alpha)\%\) confidence interval is \[ \boxed{\hat\theta \pm t_{\alpha/2,\,df}\,\mathrm{SE}(\hat\theta)} \] with \(df = n-1\) for an SRS (\(n - H\) for \(H\) strata); for large samples \(z_{\alpha/2}\) may replace \(t_{\alpha/2,\,df}\) (Section 3.4).

A.2 Simple Random Sampling

Notation: \(\hat p\) is the sample proportion of a 0/1 variable; \(e\) is the margin of error.

Mean (Section 3.2): \[ \boxed{ \hat{V}(\bar{y}) = \left(1 - \frac{n}{N}\right)\frac{s^2}{n}, \qquad \mathrm{SE}(\bar y) = \sqrt{\Big(1-\frac nN\Big)\frac{s^2}{n}}} \]

Total (Section 3.2): \[ \boxed{ \hat{t} = N\bar{y}, \qquad \hat{V}(\hat{t}) = N^2\left(1 - \frac{n}{N}\right)\frac{s^2}{n}, \qquad \mathrm{SE}(\hat{t}) = N\,\mathrm{SE}(\bar{y})} \]

Proportion (Section 3.2); the number of units with the characteristic is estimated by \(N\hat p\) with SE \(N\,\mathrm{SE}(\hat p)\): \[ \boxed{\hat V(\hat p) = \Big(1-\frac nN\Big)\frac{\hat p (1-\hat p)}{n-1}} \]

Sample size for margin of error \(e\) at level \(1-\alpha\), with \(n_0 = (z_{\alpha/2}S/e)^2\) (Section 3.5): \[ \boxed{n = \frac{n_0}{1+n_0/N} = \frac{z_{\alpha/2}^2 S^2}{e^2 + z_{\alpha/2}^2 S^2/N}} \]

A.3 Stratified Random Sampling

Notation: stratum \(h = 1,\ldots,H\) has \(N_h\) units, of which \(n_h\) are sampled by SRS; \(\pi_h = N_h/N\) is the stratum weight; \(\overline y_h\), \(s_h^2\) and \(\hat p_h\) are the sample mean, variance and proportion in stratum \(h\); \(S_h^2\) is the population variance in stratum \(h\); \(c_h\) is the cost of sampling one unit in stratum \(h\).

Estimators of the total and the mean (Section 4.2): \[ \boxed{ \hat t_{\text{str}} = \sum_{h=1}^H N_h \overline y_h, \qquad \overline y_{\text{str}} = \frac{\hat t_{\text{str}}}{N} = \sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h\overline y_h} \]

Variance and SE (\(v_h\) is the contribution of stratum \(h\); \(\hat V(\hat t_{\text{str}}) = N^2\hat V(\overline y_{\text{str}})\)) (Section 4.2): \[ \boxed{ \hat V(\overline y_{\text{str}}) = \sum_{h=1}^H v_h, \qquad v_h = \Big(1-\frac{n_h}{N_h}\Big)\pi_h^2\frac{s_h^2}{n_h}, \qquad \mathrm{SE}(\overline y_{\text{str}}) = \sqrt{\hat V(\overline y_{\text{str}})}} \]

Proportion (Section 4.4): \[ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \hat p_{\text{str}} &= \sum_{h=1}^H \frac{N_h}{N}\hat p_h, \\ \hat V(\hat p_{\text{str}}) &= \sum_{h=1}^H \Big(1-\frac{n_h}{N_h}\Big)\Big(\frac{N_h}{N}\Big)^2\frac{\hat p_h(1-\hat p_h)}{n_h-1} \end{aligned}} \]

Proportional allocation \(n_h = n\pi_h\) (Section 4.7): \[ \boxed{V_{\text{prop}}(\overline y_{\text{str}}) = \left(1 - \frac{n}{N}\right)\frac{1}{n}\sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h S_h^2} \]

Gain over SRS under proportional allocation; SSB and SSTO are the between-strata and total sums of squares of the population (Section 4.7): \[ \boxed{\text{percentage of variance reduction} \approx \frac{\text{SSB}}{\text{SSTO}} = R^2_{y\mid\text{strata}}} \]

Optimal allocation for a fixed cost (Section 4.8): \[ \boxed{ n_h = \left(\frac{N_hS_h/\sqrt{c_h}}{\sum_{l=1}^H N_lS_l/\sqrt{c_l}}\right)n, \qquad n_h \propto \frac{N_hS_h}{\sqrt{c_h}}} \]

Neyman allocation (equal costs \(c_h\)) and its variance (Section 4.8): \[ \boxed{n_h = n \frac{N_h S_h}{\sum_{l=1}^H N_l S_l}}, \qquad \boxed{V_{\text{opt}}(\overline{y}_{\text{str}}) = \frac{1}{n}\left(\sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h S_h\right)^2 - \frac{1}{N}\sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h S_h^2} \]

A.4 Ratio and Regression Estimation

Notation: SRS of \(n\) pairs \((x_i, y_i)\); the auxiliary variable \(x\) has known population total \(t_x\) and mean \(\bar x_U = t_x/N\); \(\bar x\), \(\bar y\) are the sample means; \(\hat t_y = N\bar y\), \(\hat t_x = N\bar x\).

Ratio estimators (Section 5.3.1): \[ \boxed{\hat B = \frac{\bar y}{\bar x} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n y_i}{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i} = \frac{\hat t_y}{\hat t_x}, \qquad \hat t_{yr} = \hat B\, t_x = N\,\hat{\bar y}_r, \qquad \hat{\bar y}_r = \hat B\, \bar x_U} \]

Ratio variances, with residuals \(e_i = y_i - \hat B x_i\) and \(s_e^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n e_i^2\) (Section 5.3.1): \[ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \hat V(\hat B) &= \frac{1}{\bar x^2}\left(1 - \frac nN\right)\frac{s_e^2}{n}, \\ \hat V(\hat{\bar y}_r) &= \bar x_U^2\,\hat V(\hat B) = \left(\frac{\bar x_U}{\bar x}\right)^2\left(1 - \frac nN\right)\frac{s_e^2}{n}, \\ \hat V(\hat t_{yr}) &= N^2\,\hat V(\hat{\bar y}_r) \end{aligned}} \]

Regression estimators, with the least-squares fit \(\hat B_1 = \sum(x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)/\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2\) and \(\hat B_0 = \bar y - \hat B_1\bar x\) (Section 5.4.1): \[ \boxed{\hat{\bar y}_{reg} = \hat B_0 + \hat B_1\bar x_U = \bar y + \hat B_1\left(\bar x_U - \bar x\right), \qquad \hat t_{reg} = N\hat{\bar y}_{reg} = N\hat B_0 + \hat B_1 t_x} \]

Regression variance (Section 5.4.1): \[ \boxed{\hat V(\hat{\bar y}_{reg}) = \left(1 - \frac nN\right)\frac{s_e^2}{n}, \qquad s_e^2 = \frac{1}{n-2}\sum_{i=1}^n\left(y_i - \hat B_0 - \hat B_1 x_i\right)^2} \]

Post-stratification: an SRS sorted afterwards into \(H\) groups of known sizes \(N_h\), \(\pi_h = N_h/N\); \(\bar y_h\) and \(s_h^2\) are the mean and variance of the sampled units in group \(h\), and \(n_h^{post} = n\pi_h\) is its expected sample size (Section 5.5.1): \[ \boxed{\bar y_{post} = \sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h\,\bar y_h, \qquad \hat t_{post} = N\bar y_{post}} \] \[ \boxed{\hat V(\bar y_{post}) = \sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h^2\left(1 - \frac{n_h^{post}}{N_h}\right)\frac{s_h^2}{n_h^{post}} = \left(1 - \frac nN\right)\frac1n\sum_{h=1}^H \pi_h s_h^2} \]

Domain mean: the \(n_d\) sampled units \(\mathcal S_d\) that fall in domain \(d\) have mean \(\bar y_d\) (Section 5.6.1): \[ \boxed{\hat V(\bar y_d) = \left(1 - \frac nN\right)\frac{s_d^2}{n_d}, \qquad s_d^2 = \frac{1}{n_d - 1}\sum_{i \in \mathcal S_d}\left(y_i - \bar y_d\right)^2} \]

Prediction estimator with a fitted model \(\hat y_i = \hat m(x_i)\) for every population unit (Section 5.7): \[ \boxed{\hat{\bar y}_{\text{pred}} = \frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N \hat y_i, \qquad \hat t_{y,\text{pred}} = \sum_{i=1}^N \hat y_i} \]

A.5 Cluster Sampling

Notation: an SRS \(\mathcal S\) of \(n\) of the \(N\) clusters (psus); cluster \(i\) has \(M_i\) elements (ssus), total \(t_i\) and mean \(\bar y_i\); \(M_0 = \sum_{i=1}^N M_i\); \(\bar M = \frac1n\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}M_i\) is the average size of the sampled clusters; \(s_t^2\) is the sample variance of the \(t_i\). In two-stage sampling \(m_i\) of the \(M_i\) elements are subsampled, with mean \(\bar y_i\) and variance \(s_i^2\), and \(\hat t_i = M_i\bar y_i\).

One stage, equal sizes \(M_i = M\): an SRS of cluster totals (Section 6.3): \[ \boxed{\hat t = \frac Nn\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} t_i, \qquad \mathrm{SE}(\hat t) = N\sqrt{\Big(1-\frac nN\Big)\frac{s_t^2}{n}}, \qquad \hat{\bar y} = \frac{\hat t}{NM}} \]

One stage, unequal sizes: ratio estimator of the mean per element (Section 6.5): \[ \boxed{ \hat{\bar y}_r = \frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} t_i}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} M_i} = \frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} M_i\bar y_i}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S} M_i}} \] \[ \boxed{ \mathrm{SE}(\hat{\bar y}_r) = \sqrt{\Big(1-\frac nN\Big)\frac{s_e^2}{n\bar M^2}}, \qquad s_e^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}e_i^2, \qquad e_i = t_i-\hat{\bar y}_r M_i} \]

Two stages: ratio estimator (Section 6.6): \[ \boxed{ \hat{\bar y}_r = \frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}\hat t_i}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}M_i} = \frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}M_i\bar y_i}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}M_i}} \]

Two stages: variance, a ratio piece plus \(n/N\) times a stratified piece, with \(e_i = \hat t_i - \hat{\bar y}_r M_i\) and \(M = n\bar M\) (Section 6.6): \[ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \hat V(\hat{\bar y}_r) &= \hat V_{\text{ratio}} + \frac nN\,\hat V_{\text{str}}, \\ \hat V_{\text{ratio}} &= \Big(1-\frac nN\Big)\frac{s_e^2}{n\bar M^2}, \qquad \hat V_{\text{str}} = \sum_{i\in\mathcal S}\Big(\frac{M_i}{M}\Big)^2\Big(1-\frac{m_i}{M_i}\Big)\frac{s_i^2}{m_i} \end{aligned}} \]

A.6 Unequal Probability Sampling

Notation: \(n\) independent draws with replacement (UPSWR); on each draw, unit \(i\) is selected with probability \(\psi_i = x_i/t_x\) for a size variable \(x_i\); the unit selected on draw \(j\) has ratio \(b_j^* = y_j^*/x_j^*\); \(B = t/t_x\).

Hansen–Hurwitz (HH) estimator of \(B\) (Section 7.2.1): \[ \boxed{\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}}=\bar{b}^*=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^n b_j^*}, \qquad \boxed{\widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}})=\frac{s_{b^*}}{\sqrt{n}}},\qquad s_{b^*}^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{j=1}^n\left(b_j^*-\bar{b}^*\right)^2 \]

HH mean and total: multiply the estimate and its SE by \(\bar x_U\) or \(t_x\) (Section 7.2.1): \[ \boxed{ \hat{\bar{y}}_{\mathrm{HH}}=\bar{x}_U\,\bar{b}^*,\quad \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\hat{\bar{y}}_{\mathrm{HH}})=\bar{x}_U\,\frac{s_{b^*}}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad \hat{t}_{\mathrm{HH}}=t_x\,\bar{b}^*,\quad \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\hat{t}_{\mathrm{HH}})=t_x\,\frac{s_{b^*}}{\sqrt{n}}} \]

One-stage cluster sampling with PPS: \(y_i \to t_i\), \(x_i \to M_i\), \(\psi_i = M_i/M_0\); the ratio of the cluster on draw \(j\) is its mean per element \(\bar y_j^*\) (Section 7.3.1): \[ \boxed{\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}}=\bar{b}^*=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^n \bar{y}_j^*},\qquad \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}})=\frac{s_{b^*}}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad s_{b^*}^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{j=1}^n\left(\bar{y}_j^*-\bar{b}^*\right)^2 \]

Two-stage cluster sampling with PPS: \(\bar y_j^*\) is now the mean of the \(m_j\) elements subsampled on draw \(j\); the mean per cluster and the total are \(\bar M_U\hat B_{\mathrm{HH}}\) and \(M_0\hat B_{\mathrm{HH}}\), with SEs scaled the same way (Section 7.4.1): \[ \boxed{\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}}=\bar{y}^*=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^n \bar{y}_j^*},\qquad \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\hat{B}_{\mathrm{HH}})=\frac{s_{\bar{y}^*}}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad s_{\bar{y}^*}^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{j=1}^n\left(\bar{y}_j^*-\bar{y}^*\right)^2 \]

Horvitz–Thompson (HT) estimator: without replacement, with inclusion probabilities \(\pi_i = P(i\in\mathcal S)\), for example \(\pi_i = n x_i/t_x\) (Section 7.5.1): \[ \boxed{\hat{t}_{\mathrm{HT}}=\sum_{i\in\mathcal{S}}\frac{y_i}{\pi_i}} \]
