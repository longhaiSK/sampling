A consolidated index of every numbered figure and table in the book, plus the interactive Shiny apps. Click any entry to jump to it in context.

Interactive apps

  • Interactive Demonstration (in Simple Random Sampling) — draw SRS samples one at a time or continuously, and watch the sampling distribution of \(\bar y\) accumulate against the true population mean.
  • Interactive Demonstration: Stratification by acres82 (in Stratified Random Sampling) — compare simple random, proportional, and Neyman-allocated stratified samples drawn from the same acres82-quartile population.
